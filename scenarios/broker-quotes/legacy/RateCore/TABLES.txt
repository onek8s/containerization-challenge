RateCore rate tables (Access 2000 format, Jet 4.0)
==================================================

Folder layout on a rating server (RateCore TablesPath, default D:\RateCore\Tables):

  VERSIONS.mdb   tblVersion(Product TEXT(4), Version TEXT(10), DatabaseFile TEXT(50),
                            EffectiveFrom DATETIME, EffectiveTo DATETIME NULL)
  BC2023.mdb     product tables for Business Combined 2023.x
  BC2024.mdb     product tables for Business Combined 2024.x
  ZONES.mdb      tblDistrict(District TEXT(4), FloodZone INT, SubsZone INT, CrimeZone INT)

Product databases:

  tblTrade(TradeCode TEXT(5), TradeGroup TEXT(30), MDRate DOUBLE, StockRate DOUBLE, BIRate DOUBLE,
           ELClericalRate DOUBLE, ELManualRate DOUBLE, PLRate DOUBLE, ReferFlag YESNO, DeclineFlag YESNO)
  tblFactor(Factor TEXT(20), FactorKey TEXT(20), FactorValue DOUBLE)

  Factors: CONSTRUCTION (STD/TF/NS), ROOF (STD/FLAT/THATCH/OTHER), FLOOD (1-4), SUBS (1-4),
  CRIME (1-4), ALARM (0/1/2), EXCESS (100/250/500/1000), INDEMNITY (12/18/24/36),
  PLLIMIT (1/2/5), AGE, LISTED, UNOCC, PRODUCTS, WORKAWAY (by trade group), COMPUTER, EL,
  CLAIMS (PERCLAIM, MAX), LIMIT (MDTOTAL, CRIME4STOCK, ELMANUAL, PLTURNOVER).

The tables are maintained by Pricing in Access and signed off by the Chief Actuary before each
release. The databases are opened read-only; the rating servers need read access only.
